Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · App: Shadow Escape (Android, package com.appsetz.shadowescape)

Shadow Escape is a single-player, fully offline game. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it. The short version: we do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data.

1. Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no online features. The Android app does not even request permission to access the internet.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves the following only on your own device, in the app's private storage:

  • Game progress: completed levels, stars, best completion times, relics found, coins collected, current world and last level played.
  • Settings: music and sound-effect volume, vibration on/off, graphics quality and touch-control opacity.

This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the game.

3. Device features

The game may use your device's vibration motor for haptic feedback (you can turn this off in Settings). It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, files or any other personal information.

4. Third parties

We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any. When you download the game, Google Play processes information about your download under Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy); that processing is outside the game and outside our control.

5. Children

Shadow Escape does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. There are no ads, purchases, chat or links to external websites inside the game.

6. Security

Your saved progress stays in Android's private app storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how information is handled (for example by adding an online feature), we will update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.
